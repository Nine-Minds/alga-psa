Security: secrets in getTicketFormOptions

PR #4114 · review commit @@HEAD@@

A. Finding and protection

The finding was that a caller with ticket:read could receive tenant users and clients through ticket form options, including credential columns. The current action checks ticket:read, then rejects principals outside isMspPrincipalUserType before reading option tables. User rows are selected with USER_RESPONSE_FIELD_NAMES, passed through sanitizeUserForResponse, and mapped to the seven-field ticket picker DTO. Client rows are projected to the five fields used by that picker.

Relevant code: action and guards, picker DTO mapping, and behavioral regression.
