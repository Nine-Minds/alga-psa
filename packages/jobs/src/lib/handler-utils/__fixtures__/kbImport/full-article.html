Printer Troubleshooting

Restart the spooler service, then retry the job. Run net stop spooler and read the vendor guide before escalating.

Symptoms

  • Jobs stay queued
  • Status shows offline
  • Driver reports 0x0000011b

Fix order

  1. Restart the spooler
  2. Clear the spool folder
  3. Reinstall the driver
Escalate to the vendor when the queue clears but printing still fails.
