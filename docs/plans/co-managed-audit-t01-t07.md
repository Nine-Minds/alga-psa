## T01: Product matrix

**Implemented.** [Product capabilities](../../packages/types/src/constants/productCapabilities.ts) give `co_managed` operational features and omit billing, sales, accounting, RMM, extensions and sponsorship. [Product surface rules](../../server/src/lib/productSurfaceRegistry.ts) allow operational UI/API paths and reject unsupported paths. Settings routes resolve through [the settings allowlist](../../server/src/lib/settingsProductTabs.ts), including SLA and notifications; the older route-rule row alone does not determine those routes. [Product access guards](../../shared/services/productAccessGuard.ts), operational lifecycle admission and the product-specific onboarding [permission catalog](../../server/migrations/utils/permissions/catalog.cjs) provide further enforcement. Nested sponsorship is rejected by reservation admission even if a sponsored tenant's product is incorrectly changed to PSA.

**Existing validation.** [Product surface tests](../../server/src/test/unit/product/coManagedProductSurface.test.ts) exercise the three products, operational routes/API metadata, commercial exclusions and SCIM. [Reservation integration tests](../../server/src/test/integration/coManagedReservations.integration.test.ts) verify operational role seeds, omission of commercial permissions, replay without overwriting custom settings and nested-sponsor denial. [Bootstrap integration tests](../../server/src/test/integration/coManagedBootstrap.integration.test.ts) exercise real ticket/project/document/KB/time/scheduling action and API paths, including active operation and lifecycle denial. These are substantial behavioral slices, not a complete browser matrix.

**Closed implementation findings.** Hidden navigation does not deny direct server actions. At audit discovery, `saveTacticalRmmConfiguration` in [Tactical RMM actions](../../packages/integrations/src/actions/integrations/tacticalRmmActions.ts) checked only `system_settings:update` before storing credentials and integration settings. The seeded customer Admin has that permission; neither `withAuth` nor `hasPermission` performs product admission. Commit `6c05e235a0` guards all 28 Tactical/generic RMM actions plus the Tactical background sync engines and webhook. A real PostgreSQL direct-call case and 37 Tactical unit checks pass.

The same path in [RMM status/settings actions](../../packages/integrations/src/actions/integrations/rmmIntegrationStatusActions.ts) and [RMM alert/maintenance actions](../../packages/integrations/src/actions/integrations/rmmAlertRuleActions.ts) is covered by that fix. [Teams settings actions](../../packages/integrations/src/actions/integrations/teamsActions.ts) and [telephony provider/policy actions](../../packages/integrations/src/actions/integrations/telephonyActions.ts) used availability helpers that tested only Enterprise edition and a nonempty tenant: [Teams core](../../packages/integrations/src/lib/teamsAvailabilityCore.ts), [telephony core](../../packages/integrations/src/lib/telephonyAvailabilityCore.ts). Commit `b28e476c91` adds product admission to both shared availability helpers and the EE Teams runtime. A real PostgreSQL direct-action case and 66 availability/action checks pass, independently of the release flag. Xero actions also lack an explicit product check, but their accounting permissions are absent from customer seeds; that is not the same demonstrated default-Admin bypass.

## T02: Purchase separation

**Implemented.** [The Stripe adapter](../../ee/server/src/lib/stripe/coManagedSubscription.ts) requires the configured USD 1149 monthly licensed price, a paid invoice, one dedicated subscription item, sponsor/customer ownership and current Pro sponsorship. [StripeService](../../ee/server/src/lib/stripe/StripeService.ts) exposes dedicated preview/purchase/reconciliation paths, includes monthly and annual Pro price IDs for sponsorship, and excludes co-managed subscriptions from ordinary license selection. [Purchase operations](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-purchases.ts) retain operation identity and uncertain outcomes. [Entitlement reconciliation](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-entitlements.ts) loads current provider state under the capacity lock rather than trusting stale webhook payload quantities. Purchase [actions](../../ee/server/src/lib/actions/coManagedBillingActions.ts) require account-management permission.

**Existing validation.** [Subscription tests](../../ee/server/src/__tests__/unit/coManagedSubscription.test.ts) cover owner/SKU/price/payment failures and validity bounds. [Purchase tests](../../ee/server/src/__tests__/unit/coManagedPurchases.test.ts) cover dedicated checkout, lost-response recovery, completed checkout, only-the-pool quantity changes and cancellation without canceling Pro. [Ordinary license lifecycle tests](../../ee/server/src/__tests__/unit/stripeService.licenseSubscriptionLifecycle.test.ts) exclude co-managed rows from MSP licensing. Reservation PostgreSQL cases cover duplicate purchases, pending-checkout quantities, provider observation serialization and allocation-safe reductions.

**Remaining verification.** No additional missing repository behavior was established in this review. Production must supply `STRIPE_CO_MANAGED_USER_PRICE_ID` matching the validated contract and verify paid checkout/webhook reconciliation. An explicit annual-Pro-plus-monthly-pool end-to-end case remains acceptance evidence, rather than a reason to invent an annual co-managed SKU.

## T03: Self-host claims

**Implemented.** [Signed capacity resolution](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-license.ts) requires a valid sponsor-bound Pro token and explicit `co_managed_seats`; ordinary `seats` contributes no capacity. [Reconciliation](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-entitlements.ts) rejects stale signed versions, expired/future-invalid issuance and replacement of hosted capacity. [Customer seat admission](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-admission.ts) uses customer allocations and pending invitations. [The internal user guard](../../packages/users/src/lib/internalUserLicenseGuard.ts) bypasses the installation's regular MSP counter for customer technicians. [Tenant license resolution](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/tenant-license-state.ts) retains independent tenant scope even when a customer's license expires or disappears, preventing fallback to the installation license after upgrade/restore.

**Existing validation.** [Real signature tests](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-license.test.ts) exercise sponsor binding, separate quantities, cached-result expiry and invalid tokens. Reservation PostgreSQL cases reject inflated counters, wrong sponsors, old signed reductions and incorrect entitlement sources. Bootstrap PostgreSQL cases verify tenant-bound activation, foreign/unbound/expired signatures, no revival from another installation license and unchanged installation state.

**Remaining verification.** Issuer/portal support is an explicit external dependency: verify real `aud=<sponsor>`, `co_managed_seats`, integer increasing `iat`, renewal and reduction claims before enabling self-host provisioning. The repository does not substitute an unsigned local counter. No additional missing offline admission behavior was established here.

## T04: Allocation concurrency

**Implemented.** [Reservations](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-reservation.ts) serialize the sponsor pool, generate new customer/relationship identities and retain request fingerprints. [Seat admission](../../packages/licensing/src/lib/co-managed-admission.ts) uses a common entitlement/sponsor/operation/relationship/customer lock order, counts active internal users plus distinct live invitations and prevents shrinking below committed seats. `UserService`, `UserInvitationService` and user action creation/reactivation paths invoke the shared guard. [Provisioning steps](../../packages/co-managed/src/provisioning.ts) retain completed steps and roll back failed database work without duplicating identities.

**Existing validation.** Reservation integration cases race the last allocation, duplicate operations, competing sponsors, purchase reductions and cancellation against worker steps. Bootstrap integration cases race the last account, invitation versus direct/API creation, reactivation versus creation, allocation shrink versus creation, and acceptance versus cancellation. They also verify requester exemption, expired invitation treatment, token consumption with account/role writes and directory reactivation at capacity.

**Closed finding — `c6936a8d6e`.** Cancellation now runs through the production [workflow and activity](../../ee/temporal-workflows/src/activities/co-managed-provisioning-activities.ts), authorized provisioning actions and panel. The transaction retains operation/relationship/capacity locks, deletes only an unclaimed reserved workspace, checks every registered tenant table and acknowledges cleanup before releasing capacity. Any customer user or consumed initial invitation blocks deletion, including the interval before relationship acceptance. Unknown retained tables or unexpected external resources fail closed with capacity reserved and a retryable cleanup state. Seven cleanup PostgreSQL cases exercise real bootstrap deletion, rollback/retry, signup races and idempotent release; the combined management/cleanup slice passed 15 PostgreSQL, 23 server UI and 22 EE action/workflow checks.

## T05: Topology and provisioning

**Implemented.** Reservation inputs have no existing-customer or installation target. The service generates customer identity, checks the MSP-local client, rejects nested sponsorship and enforces the one-live-sponsor constraint. [Bootstrap](../../ee/temporal-workflows/src/db/co-managed-provisioning-operations.ts) reuses tenant creation and operational seeds, creates one customer self-client/contact/service-desk board and an initial administrator invitation, and does not copy MSP logins/subscriptions/directories. [Acceptance](../../packages/co-managed/src/acceptance.ts) checks the customer's administrator, exact scope/revision and capacity before activation. [Invitation delivery](../../ee/temporal-workflows/src/activities/co-managed-provisioning-activities.ts) does not return a token to the MSP.

**Existing validation.** Bootstrap integration cases cover correct customer-owned seeds, duplicate worker delivery, unreserved factory refusal, administrator-only delivery, cancellation, concurrent acceptance, escalation-only activation without implicit board sharing, sibling/sponsor/guessed identity denial and stale permission/scope review. Reservation cases cover foreign clients, one sponsor and immutable request conflicts.

**Closed findings.** Commit `04a40a7d63` repairs the expired initial-login dead end: authorized resend/reissue renews an unused invitation through the existing invitation engine while retaining the operation, tenant, initial administrator, invitation and allocation identities. Current committed seats are checked under the seat lock before renewal; a full allocation leaves token, expiry and delivery state unchanged and sends no email. Used invitations are not reset. Nine PostgreSQL cases include real replacement-token signup and full-allocation rejection; 34 UI/status/action checks passed. Unclaimed cancellation and cleanup are implemented in `c6936a8d6e` as described in T04.

## T06: Isolation matrix

**Implemented.** [Shared-work admission](../../packages/co-managed/src/sharedWork.ts) intersects current session/API-key or notification identity, actual home roles/team membership, customer-approved scope, source resource, product/lifecycle state and home authorization policy. Qualified resource/actor IDs preserve the home session and avoid shadow users. [Ticket queue](../../packages/co-managed/src/ticketQueue.ts) and [task queue](../../packages/co-managed/src/projectTaskQueue.ts) authorize before combined search/count/pagination. Detail, conversation, attachment and notification adapters add allowlisted fields, root audience and current-read checks; native foreign-resource assumptions are not reused as customer-administrator authority.

**Existing validation.** Bootstrap integration cases cover two organizations, sibling customers, private/unshared boards and projects, explicit team membership, revoked/expired/inactive/mismatched principals, policy-lock races, source-field redactions and qualified actor collisions. Queue cases verify native/shared combined ordering/counts, denied sibling removal and redaction before search/filtering. Other cases verify portal audience admission, retained attachment download authority, live-token session binding, queued/stored notification reauthorization and API-key identity. These are real PostgreSQL behavioral tests across several fixtures; a single complete T06 browser/API acceptance matrix is still unverified.

**Closed management finding — `8bc4d0602f`.** [Management policy](../../packages/co-managed/src/managementPolicy.ts) retains the authenticated MSP's current client, board and co-management policy through the existing authorization engines. [Options and status](../../server/src/lib/actions/coManagedActions.ts) apply that policy before labels, selected-record fallback, search and pagination. Start, retry, cancellation and allocation changes authorize actual persisted client/board identities; browser adapters reject API overrides and mismatched sessions. No tenant impersonation or worker-trust widening was introduced. Eight focused PostgreSQL cases and 35 server/EE action/UI checks passed. Archive work pagination was separately corrected during the final review; see the bounded T18 review below.

## T07: Delegated administration

**Implemented — `a662785e50`.** [Delegated administration](../../packages/co-managed/src/delegatedAdministration.ts) combines explicit customer grants with the MSP's current technician assignment and home policy. Grants name a customer target and one bounded operation: board settings, ordinary-user profile fields, or resend of an existing invitation without changing its recipient or role. Delegated board writes allow only name, description and the live-ticket-timer setting; profile writes allow only first name, last name and timezone. The implementation rejects administrator targets and roles carrying privileged write permissions, and retains current role/permission locks. It provides no password/email/role reset, impersonation, credential reveal or self-grant expansion. A new acceptance still starts with no delegation; later customer grants authorize the bounded operations.

**Existing validation.** Focused PostgreSQL cases exercise actual delegated mutations, customer-only grant authority, target qualification, current role/assignment revocation, escalation prevention and invitation behavior. [The delegated UI](../../server/src/components/co-managed/CoManagedDelegatedAdministration.tsx) exposes customer grant/revoke controls and permitted MSP operations behind the existing UI release boundary. Browser actions derive the tracked home session. Broader browser acceptance remains separate from these implemented protections.

## T18: Bounded native-resource and archive review

Native customer KBs, documents and vault entries do not receive an automatic MSP grant from the relationship. [Native document actions](../../packages/documents/src/actions/documentActions.ts), document download/view routes, [KB actions](../../packages/documents/src/actions/kbArticleActions.ts) and [credential actions](../../ee/server/src/lib/actions/credentials/credentialActions.ts) derive the authenticated home tenant and use their native authorization paths. Public tenant logos are explicitly published assets, not a relationship-derived permission. No reachable native attachment path widening this relationship boundary was demonstrated in the reviewed sources.

[Archive staging](../../packages/co-managed/src/archiveFiles.ts) reads published, qualified conversation attachments after participation/audience admission. It does not crawl native document associations, library files or vault records. Shared/requester conversation bytes and the MSP's own private conversation bytes are retained separately from customer-private content. [Archive reads](../../packages/co-managed/src/archiveReads.ts) use the MSP's retained evidence and current home policy; they do not require or create a live foreign-tenant grant.

**Closed final-review findings — `febea98256`.** The task archive UI incorrectly emitted `ticketId`, and its download route always selected tickets. The corrected [route](../../server/src/app/api/co-management/archive-files/[archiveFileId]/route.ts) requires exactly one qualified ticket/task parent, tenant and relationship, rejects duplicate or unknown query parameters, and retains tracked-session admission and non-cacheable attachment responses. Archive work and history previously paginated candidate records before authorization; both now use the existing authorization-aware pagination helper and their current admission/redaction kernels, so hidden work, private notes and denied time entries cannot generate a visible page or next-page count. The file list already applies source redactions in SQL before pagination and remains unchanged. Six focused PostgreSQL cases passed across the final runs: task-route qualification, hidden-work and hidden-history paging, existing ticket downloads, archive policy and task revision history. The history fixture uses the production comment adapter to capture actual retained evidence before inserting its larger policy fixture. Nine archive UI checks also passed.

## Acceptance work versus future features

No additional repository implementation defect remains demonstrated in this bounded review after the fixes above. Production price/issuer verification and the combined multi-actor browser/API/Citus smoke matrix remain acceptance evidence; broad builds and suites were intentionally deferred under the user's faster implementation/testing direction.
