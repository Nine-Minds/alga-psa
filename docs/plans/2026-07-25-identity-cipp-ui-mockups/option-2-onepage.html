What this will and won't do

Alga reads from Microsoft

  • ✓The tenants you manage
  • ✓Users in them — name, email, phone, job title
  • ✕Never writes to Microsoft
  • ✕Never reads mail, files or calendars

Alga changes in your contacts

  • ✓Links Microsoft users to existing contacts by email
  • ✓Creates contacts for people you don't have
  • ✕Never overwrites your names, phones or titles unless you allow it
  • ✕Never deletes anything

Read-only scopes: ManagedTenants.Read.All, Directory.Read.All, User.Read, offline_access. Copy for a change record
