Before you connect

What Alga will be allowed to do

  • ✓Read the list of Microsoft tenants you manage
  • ✓Read users in those tenants — name, email, phone, job title
  • ✕Never writes anything back to Microsoft
  • ✕Never reads mail, files, calendars or Teams messages

Scopes requested: ManagedTenants.Read.All, Directory.Read.All, User.Read, offline_access. All read-only.

What happens to your contacts

  • ✓A Microsoft user is matched to an existing contact by email, within the client you mapped
  • ✓Unmatched users become new contacts
  • –Two contacts share an email? It is set aside for you to decide — never guessed
  • ✕Existing names, emails, phones and job titles are not overwritten unless you turn that on
  • ✕Nothing is ever deleted

A disabled Microsoft account marks the contact inactive. You can turn that off in step 4.

Disconnect Microsoft Entra?

Here is exactly what happens.

  • ✓Kept: all 1,842 contacts, including the 214 Alga created
  • ✓Kept: your 39 client-to-tenant matches, so reconnecting is quick
  • ✓Kept: sync history for your records
  • ✕Stops: the daily automatic sync
  • ✕Deleted: the stored CIPP credential

Contacts keep their Microsoft link, so nothing becomes orphaned. Revoke Alga's access in CIPP separately if you want the credential dead at the source too.
