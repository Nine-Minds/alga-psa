# L8b — Independent upgrade & restored-workspace activation

Reviewer question: "Can a co-managed customer leave and continue as an independent PSA tenant (hosted or self-host)?"

Base: `origin/main` @ `629c89e659d8835bc52e9e8907fc7aa37daec220` · Reference: `feature/co-managed-it` @ `2d00cb6f0e` (base `7e8d87a948`) · Branch `feature/cm-l8b-independent-upgrade-restored-workspace-ac` · Card `9815b21f`, wave 7 · Hard parents: L7a, L7b2, L8a.

## Answer to the reviewer question

**The design allows an independent conversion. This repair wires the merged L7a and L7b2 contracts into the implementation; live hosted purchase and activation demonstrations remain Smoke Test work.**

- **What the design does.** A customer administrator buys their own PSA entitlement. That is either a hosted Stripe subscription bought from `/msp/co-management/upgrade`, or, on self-host, a signed tenant-bound `pro` licence claim. The workspace then becomes a plain `psa` tenant and keeps all of its data. In the same transaction, the MSP relationship is closed and the MSP's allocation is released once. A workspace restored from a portable export is brought back the same way, with an entitlement the customer owns and a new administrator password.
- **Code that decides the upgrade.** The upgrade runs through `upgradeCoManagedRelationship` (`packages/co-managed/src/independentUpgrade.ts`). The temporal activity `product_upgrade_co_managed` calls it, and that activity runs from the product-owned `coManagedIndependentUpgradeWorkflow`. It flips `tenants.product_code` from `co_managed` to `psa` only after three things succeed inside one transaction: (1) the adapter retains a paid entitlement the customer owns, (2) the seat count covers the customer's committed seats, and (3) `coManagedUpgradeClosure.closeAndArchive` (L7a) closes the relationship.
  - The hosted adapter is `retainHostedPsaUpgradeCandidate` plus `paidPsaUpgradeFromStripe`.
  - The self-host adapter is `retainTenantPsaLicense`.
- **Gate in front of it.** Who may reach the upgrade at all is decided by `resolveCoManagedUpgradeAccess` (`packages/co-managed/src/independentUpgradeScreen.ts`). The page, its metadata, the screen action and all four mutating actions use this one function. It checks, in order:
  1. the kill switch;
  2. `release-v2-0-feature` for the tenant;
  3. `serves('co-managed')`, plus the tenant being the customer side of a relationship, plus `coManagedUpgradeClosure.available`.

  A former customer with an upgrade receipt still reaches the completed screen.
- **Closure and activation.** L7a closure/archive/release and L7b2 restore authority/suspension contracts are present on refreshed main and wired in this repair. The implementation and transaction boundaries are recorded in section 11.
- **Self-host.** Self-host additionally needs `ALGA_TENANT_LICENSE_CLAIMS=enabled` and a licence signed by the issuer, which lives outside this repo. That part is blocked-external and config-gated.

## 6. Acceptance bar

The bar is **A: upgrade flow against the Stripe emulator; relationship closure and allocation release reused from L7a; activation of a restored workspace.**

- **A1 Receipt idempotency.** The same `operationId` twice gives one closure, one release and one receipt. A changed request is refused (`independentUpgrade.test.ts`).
- **A2 Capacity only after verification.** `payment_failed` and expiry leave the tenant `co_managed` (`coManagedUpgradePaymentEvents.test.ts`, `upgradePurchaseStates.test.ts`).
- **A3 Entitlement isolation.** `subscription_kind=co_managed` and ambiguous candidates are refused (`hosted-psa-upgrade.test.ts`).
- **A4 Activation quarantine.** Activation is refused when no verified restore is pending activation, a user is active, dispatch is armed, or the source password is presented. The seam's reads are also tested against every L7b2 restore and suspension state (`portableWorkspaceActivation.test.ts`). For a departed customer, an upgrade relies only on a closure that verifies as sealed: receipt, manifest, cutoff, revision and released allocation (`departedClosureVerification.test.ts`).
- **A5 Workflow.** Without `coManaged` the workflow is unchanged. The new branch runs one step, its codes are non-retryable, and a closed gate is refused before any SQL (`product-upgrade-co-managed-activity.test.ts`).
- **A6 Gates.** The gate order holds: kill switch, flag, customer/former. Mutations need `customer`, the sponsor sync keeps reading `admits().admitted`, and the L7a closure adapter is available (`upgradeAccess.test.ts`, `coManagedUpgradeScreenAction.test.ts`, `coManagedPaymentEvents.test.ts`, `coManagedProductSurface.test.ts`).
- **Emulator flow.** `coManagedUpgradeEmulator.test.ts` retains focused injected-seam coverage. `coManagedUpgradeFullStackEmulator.integration.test.ts` runs a separate CE+EE fixture journey through signed Stripe-emulator webhook delivery and the production payment registry, then invokes the real L7a closure/finalizer and PSA initializer without mocking either.
- **Outstanding proof.** Both composed journeys are exercised against disposable PostgreSQL fixtures, including shipped-CLI restore and hosted activation. Live HTTP/UI, an actual administrator login, and the live neutrality/kill-switch walkthrough remain for Smoke Test; the application server was not started.

## 7. Review guide outline

Story: *a customer buys its own PSA and leaves co-management cleanly.* Actors: CA (customer admin), MA (MSP admin). Fixture: L7 + L8 modules, Stripe emulator.

0. Neutrality: Kansas (flag off) and Emerald (flag on, no entitlement) are unchanged; `/msp/co-management/upgrade` returns 404.
1. CA opens `/msp/co-managed` and clicks **Upgrade to AlgaPSA**.
2. The upgrade screen shows the required seats and that data is preserved.
3. Purchase with an emulator test card. Nothing changes until the webhook is verified.
4. Start the upgrade. The workspace becomes `psa`, the MSP's access closes, and the allocation is released once (the L7a receipt).
5. CA lands in AlgaPSA with their data intact.
6. Activation: the operator runs `restore-portable-workspace.ts --activate-hosted` and the receipt is printed.

Final chapter, kill switch: with `FORCE_CAPABILITIES=co-managed:off` the page returns 404, mutations are refused, the webhook is consumed without action and the activity is refused. Data is intact, then restored.
