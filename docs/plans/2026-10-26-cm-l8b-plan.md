# L8b — Independent upgrade & restored-workspace activation

Reviewer question: "Can a co-managed customer leave and continue as an independent PSA tenant (hosted or self-host)?"

Base: `origin/main` @ `85d41c2779` · Reference: `feature/co-managed-it` @ `2d00cb6f0e` (base `7e8d87a948`) · Branch `feature/cm-l8b-independent-upgrade-restored-workspace-ac` · Card `9815b21f`, wave 7 · Hard parents: L7a, L7b2, L8a.

## Answer to the reviewer question

**The design says yes. This build does not allow it yet, and that is on purpose: both paths fail closed until two hard parents are wired in.**

- **What the design does.** A customer administrator buys their own PSA entitlement. That is either a hosted Stripe subscription bought from `/msp/co-management/upgrade`, or, on self-host, a signed tenant-bound `pro` licence claim. The workspace then becomes a plain `psa` tenant and keeps all of its data. In the same transaction, the MSP relationship is closed and the MSP's allocation is released once. A workspace restored from a portable export is brought back the same way, with an entitlement the customer owns and a new administrator password.
- **Code that decides the upgrade.** The upgrade runs through `upgradeCoManagedRelationship` (`packages/co-managed/src/independentUpgrade.ts`). The temporal activity `product_upgrade_co_managed` calls it, and that activity only runs from the optional `coManaged` branch of `tenantProductUpgradeWorkflow`. It flips `tenants.product_code` from `co_managed` to `psa` only after three things succeed inside one transaction: (1) the adapter retains a paid entitlement the customer owns, (2) the seat count covers the customer's committed seats, and (3) `coManagedUpgradeClosure.closeAndArchive` (L7a) closes the relationship.
  - The hosted adapter is `retainHostedPsaUpgradeCandidate` plus `paidPsaUpgradeFromStripe`.
  - The self-host adapter is `retainTenantPsaLicense`.
- **Gate in front of it.** Who may reach the upgrade at all is decided by `resolveCoManagedUpgradeAccess` (`packages/co-managed/src/independentUpgradeScreen.ts`). The page, its metadata, the screen action and all four mutating actions use this one function. It checks, in order:
  1. the kill switch;
  2. `release-v2-0-feature` for the tenant;
  3. `serves('co-managed')`, plus the tenant being the customer side of a relationship, plus `coManagedUpgradeClosure.available`.

  A former customer with an upgrade receipt still reaches the completed screen.
- **Why nobody can upgrade in this build.** L7a (`feature/cm-l7a-departure-closure-licence-lapse`) is **not merged to main**. So `packages/co-managed/src/upgradeClosureWiring.ts` reports `available === false` and its closure methods throw. The gate therefore returns `closed` for every current customer: no page, no screen, no mutation. `prepareCoManagedUpgradePurchase` also refuses before any Stripe call, so nobody can be charged for an upgrade that the closure step would then refuse.
- **Why no restored workspace can be activated yet.** L7b2 (PR #4052, open) is **not merged**. So `portableRestoreSeam.assertInstallationAuthority` (`ee/server/src/lib/co-managed/portableRestoreSeam.ts`) throws, and activation refuses before reading the password or calling the provider.
- **What turns it on.** After L7a merges, set `available: true` and point the two methods at L7a's `closeCoManagedRelationship` / sealed-closure verifier. After L7b2 merges, plug `assertPortableRestoreInstallationAuthority` into the seam. Nothing else in L8b changes.
- **Self-host.** Self-host additionally needs `ALGA_TENANT_LICENSE_CLAIMS=enabled` and a licence signed by the issuer, which lives outside this repo. That part is blocked-external and config-gated.

## 6. Acceptance bar

The bar is **A: upgrade flow against the Stripe emulator; relationship closure and allocation release reused from L7a; activation of a restored workspace.**

- **A1 Receipt idempotency.** The same `operationId` twice gives one closure, one release and one receipt. A changed request is refused (`independentUpgrade.test.ts`).
- **A2 Capacity only after verification.** `payment_failed` and expiry leave the tenant `co_managed` (`coManagedUpgradePaymentEvents.test.ts`, `upgradePurchaseStates.test.ts`).
- **A3 Entitlement isolation.** `subscription_kind=co_managed` and ambiguous candidates are refused (`hosted-psa-upgrade.test.ts`).
- **A4 Activation quarantine.** Activation is refused when the restore marker is missing, a user is active, dispatch is armed, or the source password is presented (`portableWorkspaceActivation.test.ts`).
- **A5 Workflow.** Without `coManaged` the workflow is unchanged. The new branch runs one step, its codes are non-retryable, and a closed gate is refused before any SQL (`product-upgrade-co-managed-activity.test.ts`).
- **A6 Gates.** The gate order holds: kill switch, flag, customer/former. Nothing can start while L7a is unwired, mutations need `customer`, and the sponsor sync keeps reading `admits().admitted` (`upgradeAccess.test.ts`, `coManagedUpgradeScreenAction.test.ts`, `coManagedPaymentEvents.test.ts`, `coManagedProductSurface.test.ts`).
- **Emulator flow.** `coManagedUpgradeEmulator.test.ts` (`vitest.co-managed-upgrade.config.ts`) runs against the Stripe emulator and a real database, with L7a faked and marked `available`.
- **Exclusions.** The recorded review steps that need the real L7a closure or L7b2 restore cannot run until those cards merge. Until then, the recorded guide shows the gate closing cleanly.

## 7. Review guide outline

Story: *a customer buys its own PSA and leaves co-management cleanly.* Actors: CA (customer admin), MA (MSP admin). Fixture: L7 + L8 modules, Stripe emulator.

0. Neutrality: Kansas (flag off) and Emerald (flag on, no entitlement) are unchanged; `/msp/co-management/upgrade` returns 404.
1. CA opens `/msp/co-managed` and clicks **Upgrade to AlgaPSA**.
2. The upgrade screen shows the required seats and that data is preserved.
3. Purchase with an emulator test card. Nothing changes until the webhook is verified.
4. Start the upgrade. The workspace becomes `psa`, the MSP's access closes, and the allocation is released once (the L7a receipt).
5. CA lands in AlgaPSA with their data intact.
6. Activation: the operator runs `restore-portable-workspace.ts --activate-hosted` and the receipt is printed.

Final chapter, kill switch: with `FORCE_CAPABILITIES=co-managed:off` the page returns 404, mutations are refused, the webhook is consumed without action and the activity is refused. Data is intact, then restored.

## 8. Deployment

Image order is server, then the Temporal worker last. The `coManaged` branch is reachable only for a participating customer, and only once L7a is wired.

Flag-on prerequisites:
- L7a and L7b2 are merged and wired into the two seams;
- `STRIPE_ALGAPSA_USER_PRICE_ID` / `..._ANNUAL_PRICE_ID` are configured (otherwise no purchase UI is shown);
- for self-host, `ALGA_TENANT_LICENSE_CLAIMS=enabled` and a signed licence.

Rollback: remove PostHog targeting, then the kill switch. There is no down-migration in prod. Generated artifacts touched: the pseudo-locales (`xx`, `yy`).
