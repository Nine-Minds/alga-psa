# AlgaPSA Mobile 1.5 release notes

Status: Draft — finalize after device smoke tests  
Target version: `1.5.0` (build 30)

## Store copy

AlgaPSA Mobile 1.5 makes field work safer and sales follow-through faster.

- Work through required and optional ticket checklist items from your phone.
- Review ticket changes before applying them, with controls to suppress
  customer or internal notifications when an update should be silent.
- Capture new sales opportunities quickly and follow the current opportunity
  plan from mobile.
- See an updated Opportunity Queue with due work, quiet opportunities, and
  money-found suggestions.
